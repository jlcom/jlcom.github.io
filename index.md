Welcome to my academic website.

I am José L. Cómbita, a biologist and M.Sc. in Sciences working on community ecology, landscape ecology, biodiversity monitoring and ecological data analysis, with a particular interest in tropical ecosystems.

My research focuses on understanding how deforestation and environmental gradients affect biodiversity patterns across tropical landscapes.

### Featured Projects

<h4 style="margin-bottom: 0;">Deforestation and Biodiversity</h4>
Research on the effects of habitat loss and mammal defaunation on tropical biodiversity using dung beetles and other ecological indicators.
<h4 style="margin-bottom: 0;">Tropical Elevational Gradients</h4>
Investigation of biodiversity responses to environmental variation across tropical mountain systems.
